# makeBIMI

> makeBIMI is a free web tool for preparing and validating brand logos for BIMI. It converts supported image inputs to SVG Tiny P/S, checks SVG suitability, audits DMARC configuration, and provides a suggested BIMI DNS TXT record. It does not issue certificates or guarantee mailbox-provider logo display.

Use makeBIMI when a domain owner needs to prepare a BIMI-ready logo file, inspect a domain’s DMARC posture, or validate a remotely hosted SVG in an automated workflow. BIMI display depends on receiving-provider support and domain authentication requirements. DMARC enforcement is required for BIMI; certificate requirements vary by mailbox provider and brand type.
